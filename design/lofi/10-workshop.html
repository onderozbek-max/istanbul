10 Workshop (weapon mastery)

Specs: game-design.md 8.4, 8.15 · business-plan.md 5.2

  1. Owner request: "weapon power-ups". Each card weapon has permanent Mastery 0-10 (+4% base damage per level) and perks at M5 and M10.
  2. The cap (M5 in the slice) is visible, with the reason. Money accelerates inside the cap, never past it (power caps).
  3. Upgrade shows the exact before/after number: deterministic, no random results.
  4. Locked weapons say how to unlock them ("Beat L5").
  5. Monetization hook: Jeton + Hurda packs. "Get more Hurda" is a secondary link, not a pop-up.
