13 Grand Bazaar (daily shop)

Specs: game-design.md 8.9 · business-plan.md 5.2, 6

  1. Deterministic: every slot shows exactly what you get. This is the non-random answer to gacha chests.
  2. 6 slots per day: 4 Jeton (gold-free), 2 Altın (gold border). The Monthly Card adds a 7th.
  3. The countdown is real (daily reset at local midnight), never a fake urgency timer.
  4. Refresh: 1 free via rewarded ad per day, then 20 Altın.
  5. The shopkeeper gives the Grand Bazaar personality with rotating barks (localized TR/EN).
