Specs: prd.md US-7.1 · game-design.md 8.1, 8.10 · design-system.md 13

  1. The map is home. The route from Taksim to Ataköy is the progress bar for the whole chapter; "0.9 / 17 km" makes the journey tangible.
  2. Node states: cleared (dark, star count), current (cyan ring + Arda token), available, locked (gray), mini-boss (outlined), boss (red-bordered square). Never color alone: shape and labels too.
  3. Currency chips: Jeton, Hurda, Altın. Only Altın has a "+" (opens the store). Tapping any chip shows where it comes from.
  4. The Road Pass banner is the only promo surface on the map. Max one pop-up offer per session, never on top of the Play button (game-design.md 8.14).
  5. The tab bar has 5 items, each ≥ 48 pt. "Squad" opens Skills/Gear.
  6. The illustrated map replaces this SVG in M4; node positions follow the real geography.
